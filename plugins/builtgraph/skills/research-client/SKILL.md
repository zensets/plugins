# Research Client

## Workflow

1. Clarify the target organization, relevant geography or business unit, user's firm and role, decision context, and as-of date when not already known.
2. Resolve the legal owner, developer, parent company, operating entity, and project-specific entities without merging them solely by similar names.
3. Query active and historical projects, sectors, geographies, scale, stages, awards, and recurring project teams with Builtgraph or `$query-builtgraph`.
4. Analyze relationship concentration and potential warm paths. Treat co-participation as familiarity evidence, not a personal relationship or endorsement.
5. Use official client sources for material portfolio or leadership gaps when needed, and label those claims separately from Builtgraph evidence.
6. Use `$visualize-builtgraph` for a network, timeline, or portfolio view when it improves the decision.

## Output

Return an executive snapshot, project portfolio table, active pipeline, recurring team and incumbent patterns, relationship paths, observed procurement or delivery patterns, likely research priorities, evidence gaps, and meeting-ready questions. Include source URLs or record IDs and an explicit coverage statement.

Do not infer an upcoming procurement, budget, decision maker, or relationship that the available evidence does not establish.
