# Provisional AEC pursuit risk rubric

Use a company-supplied rubric when available. This fallback is provisional and must be labeled as such.

## Gate checks

Evaluate mandatory eligibility, required licenses, conflicts, insurance and bonding thresholds, submission deadline, mandatory forms, required experience, delivery capability, and prohibited terms. Record `pass`, `fail`, or `unknown` with an RFP page reference. Any unresolved mandatory item prevents an unconditional `Go`.

## Scored dimensions

Score each dimension from 1 to 5, where 1 is materially unfavorable and 5 is strongly favorable:

- strategic and ICP fit
- client and relationship position
- relevant project and sector experience
- competitive and incumbent position
- team completeness and partner availability
- staffing capacity and hiring burden
- scope clarity and technical risk
- schedule and delivery feasibility
- commercial, contractual, insurance, and payment risk
- pursuit effort relative to plausible value

Do not calculate dimensions for unavailable inputs. Mark them `unknown` and show how they affect the recommendation.

## Recommendation logic

- `Go`: gates pass and no material unresolved risk prevents pursuit.
- `Conditional Go`: worthwhile only if named conditions are resolved by a specified review point.
- `No Go`: a failed gate or risk-adjusted case is materially unfavorable.
- `Insufficient Information`: missing RFP content, rubric inputs, or critical facts prevent a responsible recommendation.

The score summarizes evidence; it does not override failed gates or accountable human judgment.
