# Public pursuit-review rubric

Use a company-supplied rubric when available. Otherwise organize the review without proprietary
weights or automatic decision logic.

## Gate checks

Record each material eligibility, submission, licensing, insurance, conflict, schedule, experience,
and commercial requirement as `met`, `not met`, or `unknown`, with the RFP page reference. An
unresolved mandatory requirement prevents an unconditional recommendation.

## Review dimensions

Assess strategic fit, relevant experience, client and team context, staffing information supplied by
the user, delivery feasibility, pursuit effort, and material contractual or commercial risks. For
each dimension, show evidence, confidence, and unresolved questions rather than hidden weights.

Return `Go`, `Conditional Go`, `No Go`, or `Insufficient Information` as decision support. State the
conditions and keep the final decision with the accountable reviewer.
