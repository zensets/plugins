# Research People and Career Paths

Read [../query-builtgraph/references/live-mcp-playbook.md](../query-builtgraph/references/live-mcp-playbook.md)
before live research.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the person without merging same-name identities; preserve aliases, suffixes, licenses,
   contact provenance, and ambiguous matches.
2. Inspect observed employer, career, project, filing, planning, and organization relationships that
   matter to the question.
3. Preserve the exact role and date attached to each observation. Distinguish named-on-document,
   employed-at, historical-career, applicant, consultant, attorney, and filing-professional evidence.
4. Treat repeated co-participation as observed familiarity context, not a personal relationship,
   endorsement, influence, or present employment.
5. State when freshness or incomplete history prevents a current-role conclusion.

## Output

Return an identity note, dated role and affiliation evidence, relevant projects or applications,
repeat organization or team connections, contact provenance when present, ambiguity, and the most
valuable verification step.

Do not infer current employment, authority, decision-making power, or a warm introduction from an
undated affiliation, historical role, filing appearance, or shared project.
