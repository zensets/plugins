# Trace Ownership and Financing

Read [../query-builtgraph/references/live-mcp-playbook.md](../query-builtgraph/references/live-mcp-playbook.md)
before live research.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the building, organization, or project and preserve parcel, address, and typed entity
   identifiers.
2. Collect each observed property-side role separately: permit party, borrower, lender, current
   lender, collateral link, portfolio association, and related organization.
3. Build a dated financing lifecycle where the evidence supplies originations, assignments,
   satisfactions, releases, amounts, parties, and document identifiers.
4. Follow shell-company or parent relationships only when evidence supports them. Keep unresolved
   legal control, beneficial ownership, and entity similarity explicit.
5. Check whether record freshness, missing lifecycle events, or conflicting roles could change a
   current-status conclusion.

## Output

Lead with what ownership or financing role is actually observed, followed by a dated relationship
chain and the evidence supporting each link. Separate confirmed links, plausible research leads,
conflicts, and unknown current status.

Do not treat a permit party, borrower, lender, collateral record, shared address, or portfolio
association as proof of fee ownership, parentage, beneficial control, development responsibility, or
an active unsatisfied loan.
