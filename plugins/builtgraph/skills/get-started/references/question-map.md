# Builtgraph Question Map

Use this map when the user asks what Builtgraph can investigate or when several specialist routes
could apply. The live MCP remains authoritative for current fields, filters, and coverage.

## Choose the subject

- **Building or site:** identity, address variants, parcel references, observed characteristics,
  filings, teams, ownership evidence, financing, and linked planning activity.
- **Planning application:** filing facts, public-review movement, affected buildings, named people,
  environmental obligations, and certification state.
- **Organization:** portfolio, sectors, geography, roles, clients, collaborators, buildings, recent
  owner-side activity, and related people.
- **Person:** observed employers, career paths, named applications, projects, and organization links.
- **Project:** observed participants, roles, commissioning organizations, location, sector, and
  relationship context.
- **Market or geography:** recent permit activity, recent planning movement, sector portfolios,
  firms by role, buyers, competitors, and complementary partners.

## Choose the decision

- **Research:** establish identity and the strongest relevant evidence.
- **Compare:** keep sectors, roles, geography, time windows, and coverage like for like.
- **Monitor:** define the triggering activity, lookback window, exclusions, and delivery cadence.
- **Prioritize:** use explicit evidence-backed criteria; do not imply a calibrated win probability.
- **Pursue:** combine graph context with the actual solicitation, current capacity, and user-provided
  strategy.
- **Audit:** test freshness, completeness, conflicts, pagination, attribution, and missingness before
  interpreting a result.
- **Visualize:** use a bounded network, map, timeline, or comparison only when it clarifies the
  decision.

## Important boundaries

- A historical participant is not necessarily a current team member, bidder, incumbent, or winner.
- A participant not named in inspected evidence does not establish an available commission.
- A permit filer, project LLC, borrower, portfolio association, or mailing address does not by
  itself establish current ownership, parentage, control, or development responsibility.
- Existing-building facts and historical filings do not describe a proposed development unless the
  evidence explicitly joins them.
- Recent permit and planning searches are bounded activity screens, not complete market censuses.
- Portfolio counts are observed coverage, not market share; time-filtered counts may be floors when
  dates are missing.
- A missing section, empty result, stale summary, or truncated page is not proof that the underlying
  fact or relationship does not exist.
- Citywide deed-transfer discovery, unrestricted permit-description search, private procurement
  status, internal capacity, budgets, and calibrated win probabilities require other evidence.
