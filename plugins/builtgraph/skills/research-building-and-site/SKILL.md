# Research Building and Site

Read [../query-builtgraph/references/live-mcp-playbook.md](../query-builtgraph/references/live-mcp-playbook.md)
before live research.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the site and preserve its typed ID, parcel identifiers, address variants, and unresolved
   duplicate or frontage identities.
2. Inspect the available detail index before requesting only the sections relevant to the question.
3. Separate existing-building properties, historical filings, and historical teams from any proposed
   development or planning application.
4. Follow ownership, financing, project, planning, organization, and person relationships only as
   needed for the decision.
5. Compare summary fields with detailed records and surface material date, identity, or value
   conflicts rather than silently choosing one.

## Output

Lead with a site snapshot and the decision-relevant findings. Show identifiers, current and
historical activity, observed participants, ownership and financing evidence, linked planning or
project context, conflicts, freshness, and the most useful next diligence step.

Do not turn a permit filer, borrower, lender, portfolio association, mailing address, or historical
participant into a current owner, developer, controller, or selected project team without evidence
that establishes that role.
