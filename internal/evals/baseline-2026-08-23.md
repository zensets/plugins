## Improvements demonstrated

- Rich answers now lead with the decision or ranked shortlist instead of narrating the investigation.
- Visible answers range from 438 to 597 words for supported research cases; the unsupported boundary
  answer is 77 words.
- Activity, co-participation, legal control, current loan balance, procurement, market share, and
  completeness are kept distinct.
- Adjacent findings are decision-relevant: active historical clients, role-specific competitors,
  team concentration, financing verification, and specialist diligence steps.
- The live zoning test exposed a misleading availability label. The public guidance was corrected
  and a fresh run no longer describes unnamed participants as open or available work.

## Remaining opportunities

- Improve claim-level record-ID placement in comparative firm answers without making them noisy.
- Add named person-career and RFP-with-attachment forward tests when stable test fixtures are
  available; both remain represented in the 30-question routing corpus.
- Repeat this six-case cohort after material MCP or skill changes and compare dimension-level scores,
  not just total score.
